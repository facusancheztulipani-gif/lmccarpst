Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the website lmccarpst.store, the digital books sold by LMC CARPST LLC and our internal advertising tool «Charcu Ops», a Meta app used only by our own team.

Customers of our books

When you buy a book we receive your name, email address and the payment confirmation from our payment provider. We use them only to deliver the book, answer support requests and meet tax and accounting obligations. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Charcu Ops (Meta app)

Charcu Ops is an internal tool that connects to the Meta Marketing API with a system user of our own business portfolio. It reads the performance of ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets and ads that belong to LMC CARPST LLC (spend, impressions, clicks, purchases and similar aggregated metrics) and changes the status and daily budget of those same ad sets and campaigns. It does not log in Facebook or Instagram users and it does not request permissions from people outside our team.

For the Facebook and Instagram pages of our own stores, the tool also reads the comments on our ads and posts and the messages people send to those pages, so we can answer customers and hide spam or abusive comments. We only process what people send to our pages: the public name shown by Meta, the text of the comment or message and its date. We do not read their profiles, friends or any other data.

Data obtained from Meta is stored on our own computers, only for as long as we need it to manage our ads and answer our customers, is not shared with third parties, is not sold and is not used to build profiles of people or for any purpose other than running our own stores.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion by writing to digital.ebook365@gmail.com. See also our data deletion instructions.
